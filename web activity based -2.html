⚠️ Safety Guidelines

  • Follow your guide and stay with your group.
  • Carry enough drinking water.
  • Check weather forecasts before departure.
  • Wear suitable footwear and clothing.
  • Do not litter or disturb wildlife.
  • Carry a first-aid kit and emergency contacts.
  • Do not attempt routes beyond your fitness or experience.
  • Follow local rules and turn back if conditions become unsafe.
